# CampusOne: Student Guide

Welcome to the **CampusOne** Student Portal. This guide explains the app's features and how to navigate through them.

## 📱 Application Flow

### 1. Welcome & Getting Started
When you first open the app, you are greeted by the Splash screen followed by an Onboarding flow that introduces the core values of CampusOne.

![Onboarding Screen](screenshots/student/onboarding.png)
*   **Splash Screen**: Initial loading and brand presence.
*   **Onboarding**: Swipe through highlights of the app features.

### 2. Authentication & Profile Setup
To access campus services, you need to sign in or create an account.

![Login Screen](screenshots/student/login.png) ![Register Screen](screenshots/student/register.png)
*   **Login**: Access your account with email and password.
*   **Register**: Create a new account if you are a first-time user.
*   **Complete Profile**: **Crucial Step!** Students must select their Department, Cohort, Year/Semester, Section, and Group (1-6) to see relevant schedules and assignments.

### 3. Dashboard (Home)
Your personalized hub for everything happening on campus.

![Home Screen](screenshots/student/home.png)
*   **Quick Actions**: Access Map, Assistant, and Library instantly.
*   **Upcoming Classes**: See your next lecture at a glance.
*   **Recent News**: Stay updated with the latest campus announcements.

### 4. Academic Schedule
Never miss a class or an exam again.

![Schedule Screen](screenshots/student/schedule.png)
*   **Class Schedule**: Real-time timetable filtered by your Section and Group.
*   **Exam Schedule**: View upcoming exam dates, times, and venues.

### 5. Announcements & News
Stay informed about campus events, holidays, and official notices.

![Announcements Screen](screenshots/student/announcements.png)
*   **Categories**: Filter news by Academic, Events, or General.
*   **Details**: Read full articles and view attached media.

### 6. Digital Workspace
Your virtual classroom for course materials and assignments.

![Workspace Screen](screenshots/student/workspace.png)
*   **Course List**: View all courses you are enrolled in.
*   **Assignments**: Download materials and submit your work directly through the app.
*   **Submissions**: Track the status of your submitted assignments.

### 7. Complaint Management
Report issues directly to the administration.

![Complaints Screen](screenshots/student/complaints.png)
*   **New Complaint**: Report academic issues, facility damage, or security concerns.
*   **Status Tracking**: See when your complaint is assigned, in progress, or resolved.

### 8. AI Campus Assistant
A Gemini-powered chatbot to answer your campus-related questions.

![Assistant Screen](screenshots/student/assistant.png)
*   **Instant Help**: Ask about building locations, department contacts, or academic policies.

### 9. Offline Campus Map
Navigate the campus even without an internet connection.

![Map Screen](screenshots/student/map.png)
*   **Point of Interests**: Find departments, cafes, and lecture halls.
*   **Offline Support**: Uses cached tiles to ensure you never get lost.
