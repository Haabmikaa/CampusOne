# CampusOne: Student Guide

Welcome to the **CampusOne** Student Portal. This guide explains the app's features and how to navigate through them.

## Application Flow

### 1. Welcome & Getting Started
When you first open the app, you are greeted by the Splash screen followed by an onboarding flow that introduces the core values of CampusOne.

![Onboarding Screen](screenshots/student/onboarding.jpg)

- **Splash Screen**: Gives the app time to load user data and branding assets.
- **Onboarding**: Introduces the key features students use every day.

### 2. Authentication & Profile Setup
To access campus services, a student signs in or creates an account, then completes profile details for academic filtering.

![Login Screen](screenshots/student/login.jpg)
![Register Screen](screenshots/student/register.jpg)

- **Login**: Signs in with email and password.
- **Register**: Creates a first-time account.
- **Complete Profile**: Sets department, cohort, year, semester, section, and group so schedules and assignments match the student correctly.

### 3. Dashboard (Home)
The home screen acts as the main hub for academic and campus activity.

![Home Screen](screenshots/student/home.jpg)

- **Quick Actions**: Opens tools like the map, assistant, and library faster.
- **Upcoming Classes**: Shows the next learning activity at a glance.
- **Recent News**: Highlights important campus updates.

### 4. Academic Schedule
Students can track both class schedules and exam schedules from one place.

![Schedule Screen](screenshots/student/schedule.jpg)

- **Class Schedule**: Displays the timetable filtered by section and group.
- **Exam Schedule**: Shows exam date, time, and venue when published.

### 5. Announcements & News
Important notices, academic updates, and event announcements are grouped here.

![Announcements Screen](screenshots/student/announcements.jpg)

- **Categories**: Helps students filter the type of news they want to view.
- **Details**: Opens the full message and any attached media.

### 6. Digital Workspace
The workspace is the academic area where students follow course materials and assignment activity.

![Workspace Screen](screenshots/student/workspace.jpg)

- **Course List**: Displays enrolled courses.
- **Assignments**: Lets students open instructions and submit their work.
- **Submissions**: Helps students confirm whether their work has been uploaded.

### 7. Complaint Management
Students use this screen to report issues and follow progress after submission.

![Complaints Screen](screenshots/student/complaints.jpg)

- **New Complaint**: Creates a report for academic, service, or campus issues.
- **Status Tracking**: Shows whether the complaint is pending, assigned, or resolved.

### 8. AI Campus Assistant
The assistant gives quick answers to common campus questions.

![Assistant Screen](screenshots/student/assistant.jpg)

- **Instant Help**: Supports questions about locations, services, and campus information.

### 9. Offline Campus Map
The map helps students navigate campus buildings and services.

![Map Screen](screenshots/student/map.jpg)

- **Points of Interest**: Identifies lecture halls, offices, cafes, and service areas.
- **Offline Support**: Keeps the map useful even with poor connectivity.
