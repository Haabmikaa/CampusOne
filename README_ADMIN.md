# CampusOne: Administrator Dashboard Guide

Welcome to the **CampusOne Admin Panel**. This web-based dashboard is the control center for managing users, schedules, complaints, broadcasts, and system setup across the platform.

## Dashboard Flow

### 1. Admin Login
The admin flow starts with secure sign-in to access management tools.

![Admin Login](screenshots/admin/login.png)

- **Authentication**: Protects access to sensitive campus management features.

### 2. Dashboard Overview
After login, admins land on the dashboard overview to monitor current system activity.

![Dashboard Overview](screenshots/admin/dashboard.png)

- **System Metrics**: Summarizes users, staff, and broadcast activity.
- **Recent Activity**: Surfaces the latest platform events in one place.

### 3. User & Department Management
This section controls the campus user base and its organizational structure.

![User Management](screenshots/admin/users.png)

- **Students and Staff**: Lets admins review and manage accounts.
- **Departments**: Organizes academic and service units.
- **Staff Catalogs**: Supports categories like maintenance and security for assignment flow.

### 4. Schedule Orchestration
Admins create and publish academic timing for students and staff.

![Schedules](screenshots/admin/schedules.png)

- **Class Schedules**: Builds weekly teaching timetables.
- **Exam Schedules**: Publishes official exam planning.
- **Real-Time Updates**: Pushes changes directly to the app.

### 5. Complaint & Service Resolution
Campus issues are reviewed, assigned, and tracked from this screen.

![Complaints Management](screenshots/admin/complaints.png)

- **Incident Queue**: Lists incoming complaints from the campus community.
- **Assignment Flow**: Sends issues to the correct staff category.
- **Resolution Tracking**: Monitors status and follow-up.

### 6. Broadcasts & Emergency Alerts
Admins can communicate with the full campus or targeted audiences.

![Broadcasts](screenshots/admin/broadcasts.png)

- **Broadcast Messages**: Delivers important announcements.
- **Urgent Alerts**: Supports fast communication during critical situations.

### 7. Assignment Configuration
This page manages the academic grouping used by students and schedules.

![Assignment Setup](screenshots/admin/assignments.png)

- **Sections and Groups**: Configures cohort distribution such as 6 sections and 6 groups.

### 8. Events & Task Tracking
Admins can organize campus activities and internal operational work.

![Events and Tasks](screenshots/admin/events-tasks.png)

- **Events**: Tracks seminars, workshops, and campus programs.
- **Tasks**: Helps follow internal work items across the admin team.

### 9. System Support & Settings
Platform-level options and support controls are managed here.

![Settings and Support](screenshots/admin/settings-support.png)

- **System Settings**: Controls app-wide configuration and behavior.
- **Support Area**: Helps admins access assistance and operational tools.
